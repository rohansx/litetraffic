For CyberSource specifically, they return a special error code 235 `The requested capture amount exceeds the originally authorized amount`, see page 351 of [Credit_Cards_SO_API.pdf](http://apps.cybersource.com/library/documentation/dev_guides/CC_Svcs_SO_API/Credit_Cards_SO_API.pdf). On that same guide, page 47, you can see that some specific processors require the captured amount to be less than or equal to the authorized amount, but it is unspecified for others.

Finally, this behavior is probably not enforced in the sandbox environment.
